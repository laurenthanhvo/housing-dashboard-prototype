Current validated sources

HCD APR and RHNA 6th Cycle progress, California DOF E-5, Census/ACS, HUD FY 2026 income limits, and aggregated NHPD preservation data.

Important limitations

APR data are jurisdiction-reported and may be revised. ACS values are survey estimates. DOF and Census/ACS housing-stock estimates use different methods. NHPD assisted-unit counts are validated conservative estimates where subsidies overlap.

The public dashboard uses aggregated NHPD results. Property-level NHPD data are not redistributed.
