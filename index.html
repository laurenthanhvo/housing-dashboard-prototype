Version 1 includes

APR permits/completions, RHNA progress and targets, ACS/DOF context, jurisdiction geography, and SANDAG zoning as context.

Version 1 does not include

A real-time regional permit tracker, parcel-level rent roll, precise unit-level rent pricing, forecasting, or sensitive address-level preservation-risk data.

Sensitive preservation-risk datasets should be mapped only at aggregated geographies unless redistribution rights and privacy rules are clear.
