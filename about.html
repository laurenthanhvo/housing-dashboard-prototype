About

A clearer first stop for San Diego housing data

Housing data for San Diego exists across state, regional, county, municipal, and federal systems. This prototype brings the most feasible first-version sources into one dashboard so policymakers, planners, researchers, journalists, students, and residents can quickly understand housing supply, RHNA progress, and basic context indicators.

Purpose

Make housing supply status and gaps easier to understand without requiring users to navigate several separate data portals.

Audience

Designed for policymakers, housing and land use professionals, planners, academics, journalists, students, and interested residents.

Design principle

Every chart should pair a clear takeaway with a source, date range, refresh note, and one plain-language caveat.
